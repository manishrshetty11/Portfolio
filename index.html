PROBLEM

Booking details were reachable, but the path back through booking history looked unusually weak for Puja users. The team needed to know whether this was a re-entry friction signal.

METHOD & EVIDENCE

Linked app/web events to booking cohorts in BigQuery and Superset. The booking-history list visit rate trailed Chadhava by 46–71 percentage points, while 73–82% of Puja users still reached booking details.

DECISION & NEXT TEST

Informed a CRM deep-link review. A next experiment would compare direct booking-detail links with the current route on revisit and repeat-booking behavior; no uplift is claimed here.

Read the incrementality case +
PROBLEM

DA was growing GSV, but leadership needed to distinguish genuinely incremental orders from existing customers shifting what they bought.

METHOD & EVIDENCE

Segmented 10M+ users into Non-DA, first-time DA, and repeat DA groups across six lifetime-value segments. Applied causal additive decomposition using February 2026 as the pre-program baseline.

DECISION & NEXT TEST

Quantified 14,338 incremental orders/day: 78% from first-time adoption and 22% from behavioral lift. The framework gave leadership an investment lens; a future holdout would sharpen the causal estimate.
